Product name: Proofloom

Tagline (42/60 characters):
Catch packaging discrepancies before print

Short description (215/260 characters):
Proofloom is building a review workspace for packaging teams. Explore a sample demo that compares artwork with approved specifications, links discrepancies to sources, and tracks corrections through human re-review.

## First-maker comment (for the confirmed maker to review)

Hi Product Hunt! Proofloom is being built for the people who give packaging its final check: packaging managers, artwork coordinators, quality teams and printers.

A product quantity can change in a specification while the artwork keeps the old value. We want to make that difference easier to find, explain and correct before printing.

The current interactive demo uses a fictional hand-wash label. You can inspect a quantity mismatch, a product-code discrepancy and missing storage wording, assign a sample reviewer, leave a local comment and move a finding into re-review. The demo runs locally in your browser; it does not process uploaded files or connect to live AI services.

Proofloom is still being built. The intended product assists human review rather than certifying compliance or automatically approving packaging. We would welcome feedback on the sample workflow, especially how your team handles revisions and final print decisions.

What is the hardest discrepancy to track in your current packaging review process?
